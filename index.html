Crypto Arbitrage Scanner

Funding Rate Arbitrage

Cross-exchange funding carry scanner over 15 perp venues. Identifies spot+perp and perp+perp routes, ranks by cost-adjusted net APR, and simulates a portfolio with per-exchange capital limits and PnL tracking.

Funding Drift

Scanner, backtester and live simulator comparing cumulative funding cost against cumulative realized returns over rolling windows, to find perp markets where the losing side is under forced-close pressure.

Python aiohttp Backtesting

Polymarket BTC Options

Prices Polymarket BTC 5-minute binaries with a TWAP-aware cash-or-nothing N(d2) against live Binance volatility. Over 230 settled windows and 460K real fills the market's own price out-forecast the model, and what survived costs was feed latency, not the model. Now running a paper A/B of the original taker against three passive makers on the same windows.

Python Black-Scholes WebSockets

Polymarket Whale Tracker

Scans Polymarket 5-minute BTC binary markets for wallets that consistently profit on late-flipping outcomes. A seven-day sweep covered 2,010 markets and 555K trades across 22K wallets, scoring each account on win rate, trade timing relative to close, and co-movement with other accounts.

Python asyncio SQLite Rich

Prediction Market Maker

Kalshi market-making bot. v1 (Avellaneda-Stoikov on niche markets) lost ~$130 over ~4,400 paper fills, all of it in exits that crossed the spread; passive round trips were the only profitable path. v2 never crosses, skews an edge curve against inventory, holds to resolution, and adds a Binance-anchored book on Kalshi's hourly BTC/ETH strikes. Both run side by side in paper.

Python Avellaneda-Stoikov Kalshi API

Government Auction Scanner

Mispricing scanner across eleven federal and county asset-disposal venues -- surplus, seized, foreclosed. No venue publishes a value anchor, so rather than trusting a headline discount it establishes its own fair value from comps (or an AI estimate grounded in retrieved comps, flagged at lower confidence) and computes the break-even max bid net of frictions on both sides: buyer premium, tax and freight to acquire; commission, carry and repairs to exit.

Python Claude API Click Rich
